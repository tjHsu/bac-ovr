Returns & refunds 退換貨規則

依中華民國《消費者保護法》第19條及相關規定辦理。
We follow Article 19 of Taiwan’s Consumer Protection Act and related rules.

  1. 1

    七天猶豫期 · 7-day cooling-off

    收到作品後七天內（含例假日），您可以不附理由、不負擔任何費用申請退貨。猶豫期不是試用期，請妥善保管作品。

    You can return a work within 7 days of receiving it (weekends and holidays included), with no reason needed and at no cost to you. This is a cooling-off period, not a trial period, so please take good care of the work.

  2. 2

    如何申請 · How to return

    請在七天內以 Email 或電話告訴我們訂單姓名與作品名稱（退貨原因可不填）。收到通知後，我們會安排取件。

    Within the 7 days, email or call us with the name on the order and the work’s title (a reason is optional). Once we hear from you, we’ll arrange collection.

  3. 3

    作品狀態 · Condition

    請連同畫框、包裝材料與作品保證書一併退回。為檢查作品而拆封不影響退貨權利；若作品有超出檢查所需的使用或損壞，我們會依損壞程度與您協商修復費用。

    Please send the work back with its frame, packing materials and certificate of authenticity. Unpacking it to look it over doesn’t affect your right to return. If the work has been used or damaged beyond inspection, we’ll agree a repair cost with you based on the damage.

  4. 4

    退貨運費 · Return shipping

    在七天猶豫期內退貨，取件與運送費用由潛地工作室負擔。

    For returns within the 7-day period, Dachshund Studio pays for collection and return shipping.

  5. 5

    退款時間 · Refunds

    收到退回作品後 15 天內，我們會以原付款方式全額退款。

    We refund the full amount to your original payment method within 15 days of receiving the returned work.

  6. 6

    運送受損 · Damaged on arrival

    若作品送達時有損壞，請保留外箱與包裝並拍照，盡快與我們聯繫。原創作品僅此一件，無法換貨，我們會提供修復或全額退款。

    If a work arrives damaged, keep the box and packing, take photos and contact us as soon as you can. Originals are one of a kind and can’t be swapped, so we’ll offer a repair or a full refund.

  7. 7

    不適用情形 · Exceptions

    依《通訊交易解除權合理例外情事適用準則》，依您要求客製或委託創作的作品不適用七天猶豫期；我們會在接受委託前事先告知。

    Under Taiwan’s rules on reasonable exceptions for distance sales, works commissioned or customised at your request aren’t covered by the 7-day period. We’ll tell you before we accept a commission.
